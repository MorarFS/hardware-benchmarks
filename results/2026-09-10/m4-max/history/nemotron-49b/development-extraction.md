**D-Q1: How many prisoners were selected for execution, and did all seven die?**

Seven prisoners were selected for execution. Not all seven died as stated; four were beheaded, and three were initially hung but survived due to a bungled execution. 
Quotation: "Seven were selected for execution, and four of them were duly beheaded... The last three rioters were to be hung, but the hang-man so bungled his task that two of the criminals... fell to the ground alive." [PDF p. 102]
