H3-Q1: Constantine was called Porphyrogenitus because he was born in the Purple Chamber, the room in the palace set aside for the Empress. This name was given him because he was born in the Purple Chamber [PDF p. 241].

H3-Q2: Saracen pirates captured Thessalonica in 904, but they did not retain it as they fled with their booty after the city's fall. The capture of the second city of the empire by a fleet of African adventurers was an incident disgraceful to the administration of Leo [PDF p. 242].

H3-Q3: Romanus Lecapenus was removed by the mob and the guards, and his sons were then expelled by Constantine. The mob and the guards joined to sweep away the presumptuous Stephen Lecapenus and his brother [PDF p. 243].

H3-Q4: Leo recommended caution rather than rash general battle against the men of the West, the Franks and Lombards. It is only when dealing with the men of the West, the Franks and Lombards, that Leo recommends caution [PDF p. 245].
