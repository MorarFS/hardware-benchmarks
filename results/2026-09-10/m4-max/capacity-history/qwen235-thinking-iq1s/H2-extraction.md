H2-Q1: Who intervened in the gladiatorial games, in what year, and what happened to him?
The monk Telemachus intervened in the gladiatorial games in A.D. 404, and "There was an angry scuffle, and the good monk was slain" [PDF p. 171].

H2-Q2: Who made infanticide a capital offence, and when?
Valentinian I made infanticide a capital offence in 374, as stated: "Valentinian I. in 374 assimilated infanticide to other forms of murder, and made it a capital offence" [PDF p. 171].

H2-Q3: Did the passage claim Christianity immediately ended slavery? State its qualification.
The passage states that "Though slavery was not extinguished for centuries" [PDF p. 173], indicating Christianity did not immediately end slavery.

H2-Q4: Why did Oman regard widespread ascetic monasticism as politically dangerous?
Oman regarded widespread ascetic monasticism as politically dangerous because "When men retire from their duties as citizens... cease to be of the slightest use to society" [PDF p. 174].
