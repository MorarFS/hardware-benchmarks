Constantine dedicated his new capital in May 330 AD [PDF p. 54]. The Hippodrome stood in an open space which the Turks call the Atmeidan [PDF p. 51]. Constantine's porphyry column in the forum featured a bronze image [PDF p. 51]. The city featured monuments like the church of St. Sophia [PDF p. 51]. Constantine attracted many immigrants through special privileges and corn distribution [PDF p. 55]. The wheat tribute from Egypt was transferred to the new capital [PDF p. 55].
