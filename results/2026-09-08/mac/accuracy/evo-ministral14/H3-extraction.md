**H3-Q2: Which city did Saracen pirates capture in 904, and did they retain it?**
Saracen pirates captured Thessalonica in 904, but they did not retain it; they fled with their booty after taking the city.
*Supporting quotation:* "The capture of the second city of the empire by a fleet of African adventurers was an incident disgraceful to the administration of Leo, and caused much outcry and sensation. But it is fair to say that it was taken almost by surprise, and stormed from the side of the sea where no attack had been expected... When they had taken it the Saracens fled with their booty, and made no attempt to hold its walls." [PDF p. 242]

**H3-Q4: Against which opponents did Leo recommend caution rather than rash general battle?**

*Supporting quotation:* "It is only when dealing with the men of the West, the Franks and Lombards, that Leo recommends caution and deprecates any rash engagement in a general action, preferring to wear the enemy down by cutting off his supplies and harassing his marches." [PDF p. 245]
