H1-Q1: Who led the Visigoths as they retreated toward the Danube?
Fritigern led the Visigoths. [PDF p. 62]

H1-Q2: What two conditions did Valens impose for admission, and what happened to the arms condition?
Valens required the Goths to give hostages and surrender their arms. The Goths were allowed to keep their arms after bribing Roman officials. [PDF p. 63]

H1-Q3: According to this passage, how did Lupicinus’s food policy contribute to revolt?
Lupicinus withheld food from the Goths, selling it at exorbitant prices, which drove them to desperation. [PDF p. 63]

H1-Q4: What result does the passage give for the battle at Ad Salices?
The battle at Ad Salices was a desperate fight with no decisive result. [PDF p. 64]

H1-Q5: What was the exact number of Roman soldiers killed at Ad Salices?
The passage states that forty thousand Roman soldiers were killed at Adrianople, not Ad Salices. [PDF p. 66]
