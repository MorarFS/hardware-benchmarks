H2-Q1: Who intervened in the gladiatorial games, in what year, and what happened to him?
The monk Telemachus intervened in the gladiatorial games in AD 404. He was slain during an angry scuffle. [PDF p. 172]

H2-Q2: Who made infanticide a capital offence, and when?
Valentinian I made infanticide a capital offence in 374. [PDF p. 173]

No, the passage states that Christianity did not immediately end slavery, but it "immensely modified" its evils. [PDF p. 173]

Oman believed widespread ascetic monasticism was politically dangerous because it led men to neglect their duties as citizens. [PDF p. 175]
