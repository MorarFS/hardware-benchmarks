H4-Q1: Which ruler reunited the surviving Ottoman realm after the civil wars?
Mohammed, the youngest of the sons of Bayezid, was a man of genius: he triumphed over the last of his brothers, and united all the remnants of the Ottoman realm that remained. [PDF p. 362]

H4-Q2: Why did Manuel’s support for the two Mustaphas lead to war with Murad?
Manuel tried to make trouble for Murad, by supporting against him two claimants to the Ottoman Sultanate, each named Mustapha, one the uncle, the other the brother of the new ruler.  This drew down on the empire the fate which had been delayed since 1370: the Sultan declared war on Manuel, [PDF p. 365]

H4-Q3: What immediate development caused Murad to lift the siege?
At last the pretender Mustapha, whom Manuel had supplied with money to cause a revolt against his brother, began to stir up such trouble in Asia Minor, that the Sultan determined to raise the siege and march against him. [PDF p. 365]

H4-Q4: What role does the passage assign to the vision of the Virgin?
The report of a miraculous vision of the Virgin, who vouchsafed to reveal herself as the defender of the city, encouraged the Greeks to resist with a better spirit than might have been expected. [PDF p. 365]
